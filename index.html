Personal Job Applicant

A private, single-user desktop application that discovers relevant jobs, evaluates verified professional evidence, prepares truthful application documents, and sends applications only under the account owner's explicit authorization.

The application runs locally on its owner's computer. This website provides information about it; it has no sign-up, mailbox access, application forms, analytics, or downloadable personal records.

Google account access

Gmail send permission lets the application send an approved application from its owner's mailbox. Gmail metadata permission verifies the sender account and the resulting SENT message. Google consent does not authorize sending job applications: a separate exact application approval or an explicitly authorized standing policy is required.

The owner may also authorize a separate read-only connection for subscribed job-alert emails. Application delivery and alert reading use separate locally stored credentials.

Controls

Application policies restrict recipients, employers, eligibility, evidence quality, freshness, and application counts. Duplicate and uncertain transactions are blocked. A Gmail SENT record proves mail-service acceptance, not delivery to an employer or employer acknowledgement.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. The operator is also the sole user; the support contact is shown on Google's consent screen.
