Terms of use

Effective 18 November 2026. Personal Job Applicant is a private, single-user tool, not a public recruitment service. No third-party account access or public registration is offered.

Authorization

Google account consent permits the requested API access. Sending an employer application additionally requires the owner's exact application approval or an explicitly authorized standing policy. Testing permission does not authorize employer applications.

Permitted use

Use only accurate, verified candidate information and application channels permitted by the relevant employer and service. Do not bypass login, CAPTCHA, platform restrictions, or access controls. Unsupported or uncertain workflows must stop for reconciliation.

Outcomes and limitations

Job suitability is an evidence-based assessment, not a guarantee of eligibility acceptance or employment. Mail-service acceptance, ATS acknowledgement, and employer confirmation are distinct outcomes. Automated operation depends on account access, service availability, the local computer, and current authorization.

The owner may disable policies or revoke Google access at any time. See the privacy policy for storage and deletion controls. The support contact is shown on Google's consent screen.
