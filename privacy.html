Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Personal Job Applicant is a private desktop application operated by and for one account owner.

Information used

The application uses the owner's resume, confirmed candidate details, repository evidence, job descriptions, application drafts, authorization records, and submission history. Gmail delivery uses the authenticated account address and the headers and labels of messages sent by the application to verify their outcome. It does not read message bodies through the delivery connection.

A separately authorized read-only Gmail connection may read subscribed job alerts for job discovery. It is not required for sending applications. Google permissions are requested through Google's consent screen.

Purpose and sharing

Google user data is used only for the requested job discovery, authorized application delivery, and outcome verification features. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Application messages and attachments go through Gmail only to recipients covered by explicit application authorization. Professional evidence and public job descriptions may be sent to the owner's configured AI provider for assessment and preparation when authorized. Delivery metadata and OAuth credentials are not supplied to the AI evaluator.

Storage and retention

OAuth refresh credentials are stored in the owner's macOS Keychain. Candidate records, drafts, and receipts are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are excluded from source control. They remain until the owner removes them. Messages sent through Gmail remain subject to the owner's Gmail retention and deletion settings.

This public information site contains no candidate records, tokens, or application documents and uses no analytics or cookies of its own. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request logs under its own privacy policy.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke access in Google account connections, remove the application's Keychain entries, disable application policies, and delete local records. Revoking access does not retract messages already sent. Deleting application history removes local duplicate-prevention evidence, so submissions should remain disabled until that history is reconciled.

For this single-user application, the operator is also the sole user. The support contact is shown on Google's consent screen. Changes in Google data use require updated disclosure and renewed consent where applicable.
